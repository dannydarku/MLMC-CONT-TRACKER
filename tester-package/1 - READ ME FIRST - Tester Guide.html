Tester Guide

Contract & Document Tracker · Prototype

1. What you are testing

This is an early prototype of a system for tracking hospital contracts and controlled documents. Every record has an owner, a status, key dates (expiry, renewal notice, review) and a full activity history. Two kinds of users exist:

  • Records Team full access: create, edit, delete, approve access requests, manage users.
  • Limited can open records they own; everything else needs approval from the Records Team before they can view or download it.
All data is sample data.Do not enter real contracts, patient information or personal details. Vendors and most names are made up.

2. How to open it

  1. Unzip the file first. Right-click the .zip → Extract All… → Extract. (It won't work if you open it from inside the zip.)
  2. In the extracted folder, double-click “2 - OPEN THE APP.html”.
  3. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer. Nothing to install.
Good to know Everything you do is saved only in your own browser on your computer. Other testers have their own separate copy, so they won't see your changes, and you won't see theirs.
To act as a different person, click Sign out (top right) and sign in as someone else. Changes you made carry over, so you can request access as one user and approve it as another.
To start over at any time: sign in as a Records Team user → Settings & Integrations → Reset demo data.

4. Test scenarios

Work through these in order. Tick each step as you go (ticks are not saved; print the page if you want a paper copy). If anything behaves differently from the green “Expected” note, report it (section 5).

A. Sign-in and dashboardas Andrea (Records Team)
  • Try signing in with a wrong password, then with the correct one.
    Wrong password shows an error; correct one opens the dashboard.
  • Look at the summary cards and the “Needs attention” list. Click a card and a row.
    Cards open the matching list; rows open that record. Red = needs action now, amber/blue = coming due.
  • In “By department”, click a department.
    Registry opens showing only that department's records.
B. Registry: find recordsas Andrea
  • Search for “MRI”, then for a code such as MLMC-NSG.
    List narrows as you type.
  • Combine filters: Category, Department, Status, Monitoring. Then click Clear.
    Results match every filter; Clear shows all records again.
  • Click Export CSV and open the file in Excel.
    A spreadsheet of the records currently shown.
C. Create a recordas Andrea
  • Click + New record. Change Type, Department and Version and watch the Document code.
    Code updates automatically, e.g. MLMC-RAD-CSA-001-1.0.
  • Switch Type between a document (e.g. Policy) and a contract (e.g. Service Agreement).
    Documents ask for a next review date; contracts ask for expiry date, notice period, counterparty and value.
  • Try saving with the owner or key date missing, or with a code that already exists.
    A clear message explains what is missing; nothing is saved.
  • Create a contract that expires in about 20 days.
    It appears in the Registry and on the dashboard flagged red (“Expires in … d”).
D. Work with a recordas Andrea
  • Open any record. Click View document, then Download.
    A cover-page preview opens; a small sample PDF downloads.
  • Click Edit, change the title and owner, save. Then use Change status.
    Changes show immediately and appear in the record's Activity tab with who/when/what.
  • Click Upload file and attach a small PDF or image (under 300 KB), then download it. Also try a file over 300 KB.
    Small file uploads and downloads back; large file shows a size message.
  • Delete the record you created in scenario C.
    Asks for confirmation; record disappears; the deletion is listed in Activity Log.
E. Request access (limited user)sign out → sign in as Joanna
  • Check the menu and dashboard.
    Fewer menu items; dashboard shows only Joanna's records and requests.
  • Open one of Joanna's own records (e.g. Patient Fall Prevention Protocol).
    Opens without approval because she is the owner.
  • In Registry, open a record marked Locked (e.g. MRI Scanner contract). Request View only with a reason.
    Only basic details are shown; after sending, it says “Request pending”.
  • Request access to a second locked record choosing View & download.
    Both requests appear under My Requests → Pending.
F. Approve, reject, revokesign out → sign in as Andrea
  • Go to Access Requests. Approve one of Joanna's requests and reject the other (a reason is required).
    Approved one moves to Active grants with an expiry date; rejected one moves to History.
  • Sign in as Joanna again and open the approved record.
    Opens with a “Temporary access” note. Download is only available if download was approved. The rejected record shows the rejection reason.
  • As Andrea, Revoke Joanna's active grant, then check again as Joanna.
    The record is locked again.
G. Users and rolesas Andrea
  • Settings & Integrations → Users & roles: click Grant full access for Ramon.
    Asks for confirmation; Ramon becomes Records Team; logged in Activity Log.
  • Sign in as Ramon and check the menu. Then (as Andrea) Remove full access from Ramon.
    Ramon gets the full menu, then goes back to limited.
  • Check you cannot change your own access, and cannot remove the last Records Team member.
    Your row says “You”; the last full-access person can't be removed.
H. Settings and monitoring rulesas Andrea
  • Change “Flag expiries / reviews” from 90, 60, 30 to 120, 60, 14 and save. Revisit the dashboard.
    Flag colours and counts change to match the new thresholds.
  • Fill in the OneDrive fields and click Connect OneDrive.
    A message explains it is not connected in this prototype (expected for now).
I. Free testing: try to break itany user
  • Very long titles, special characters (< > & " '), emojis, past and far-future dates.
  • Click quickly / double-click buttons, use the browser Back button, refresh in the middle of a task.
  • Make the browser window narrow (or use a phone-sized window) and check pages still read well.
  • Anything confusing: wording, layout, missing information you'd expect to see.

Tester name: Browser (Chrome / Edge): Scenario + step (e.g. F-2): What I did: What I expected: What actually happened: Screenshot attached? (Y/N) How serious? (Blocker / Annoying / Minor / Suggestion)

6. Known limits of this prototype

Please don't report these as bugs. They are planned for a later version:

  • No real accounts: sign-in uses the demo accounts above (later: hospital Microsoft 365 sign-in).
  • Data is not shared between testers or computers, and clearing your browser data erases it.
  • No email notifications.
  • OneDrive is not connected; files are small sample PDFs or uploads up to 300 KB.
